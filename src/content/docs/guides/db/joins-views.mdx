Rljson stores a catalog normalized: every aspect of a car lives in a layer
of its own, every value once. A view puts the aspects back side by side: one
row per car, one column per value. Db builds it with a join.

<UsecaseCar />

The Velora Amita above is one row of such a view: its price comes from the
prices layer, brand and model from the brands layer, the workshop from the
workshops layer, and the owner from the workshop.

You will learn how to:

- describe the columns of a view with a `ColumnSelection`
- join a catalog into a view
- follow references from a component to other components
- join the bill of materials of every car

## What you have learned

- A `ColumnSelection` describes a view: an alias and a route per column.
- `db.join` turns a catalog into one row per car.
- Routes continue through reference columns into other tables.
