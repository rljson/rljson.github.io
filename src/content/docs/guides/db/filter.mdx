A catalog lists its cars, a view shows one row per car. Often you only need
some of them: the expensive cars, the diesels, the SUVs. A `RowFilter`
selects them.

The figure shows a catalog of four cars. The view below has five, the cars
of Velora: Amita, Orveo, Calix, Rivuna and Eldel.

You will learn how to:

- filter a column by a number
- filter a column by a string
- combine filters with `and` and `or`

## 2. Filter by a number

A column filter names the route of its column, a type, an operator and the
value it searches for. Number filters know `equals`, `notEquals`,
`greaterThan`, `greaterThanOrEquals`, `lessThan` and `lessThanOrEquals`.

## What you have learned

- A column filter names a column by its route and compares its value.
- A `RowFilter` combines column filters with `and` or `or`.
- `join.filter` narrows a view, `join.clone` keeps the original.
